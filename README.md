# Miral✦AirCanvas

> Draw in the air. No stylus, no touch screen — just your hand and a webcam.

A browser-based computer vision app that tracks your hand in real-time and turns your index finger into a paintbrush — no hardware required.

---

## ✦ Demo

> ☝️ One finger → draw  
> ✌️ Two fingers → lift the pen  
> ✊ Closed fist → stop  

---

## ✦ Features

- Real-time hand tracking via **MediaPipe Hands**
- Draw freely in the air using your **index finger**
- Multiple brush **colors** and adjustable **size**
- Live hand **skeleton overlay**
- **Save** your drawing as PNG
- Runs entirely in the browser — no installs, no backend

---

## ✦ Built With

| Technology | → | Purpose |
